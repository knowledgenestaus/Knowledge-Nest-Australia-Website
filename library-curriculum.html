Curriculum: Free lesson materials

Openly available lesson materials, scope and sequences, decodable readers and guides. Australian collections come first, from Victorian Lesson Plans and the Literacy Hub to Connective Fluency, followed by Core Knowledge, Oak National Academy, TextProject and more from overseas. Every entry links to the original.

Australian 15

Written for Australian classrooms, by Australian teachers, organisations and education departments.

Brad Nguyen

Connective Fluency (opens in a new tab)

By Brad Nguyen · Maths: Number facts and computation · Years 1-6 · Australian Curriculum

A free maths fluency program built on three short routines, namely whole-class incremental rehearsal, paired fact practice and timed computation practice. The slides, booklets and diagnostic assessments are sent once you fill in a short form.

FreeOpens at bradnguyen.com

SPELD NSW's guide to the free decodable books you can download or read on a screen, with a link to each collection, plus libraries that lend decodable sets. Handy to pass on to families.
