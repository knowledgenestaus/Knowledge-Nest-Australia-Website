Voices: People and organisations to follow

The researchers, teachers, writers and organisations whose work I keep coming back to, in Australia and beyond. Each links to their own site, Substack or podcast. Every entry links to the original.

AERO (opens in a new tab)

Research and practice guides · Australia

The Australian Education Research Organisation, Australia's national education evidence body. It publishes practice guides, explainers and classroom videos on how students learn and which teaching practices work.

Opens at edresearch.edu.au

Alex Blanksby (opens in a new tab)

Substack · Australia

Writes VIC Maths Notes, a Substack on teaching maths explicitly and what the evidence means for Victorian classrooms.

Opens at vmnalex.substack.com

Alison Clarke (Spelfabet) (opens in a new tab)

Blog · Australia

Melbourne speech pathologist whose Spelfabet site has long been a first stop for phonics and spelling. Her blog explains the research in plain language and reviews resources.

Opens at spelfabet.com.au

AUSPELD (opens in a new tab)

Guides and resources · Australia

The Australian federation of SPELD associations, supporting children and adults with learning difficulties and specific learning disabilities. It publishes the Understanding Learning Difficulties guides for teachers and parents.

Opens at auspeld.org.au

Brad Nguyen (opens in a new tab)

Free maths program · Australia

Australian educator who created Connective Fluency, a free maths fluency program for Years 1 to 6 built on whole-class rehearsal, paired fluency practice and timed computation.

Opens at bradnguyen.com

Brendan Lee (opens in a new tab)

Substack and podcast · Australia

Australian teacher who started in high schools and returned to the profession as a primary teacher. He hosts the Knowledge for Teachers podcast and writes about teaching and school improvement.

Opens at knowledgeforteachers.substack.com

Bron Ryrie Jones (opens in a new tab)

Conversations and podcast · Australia

Works with schools and systems on explicit, responsive teaching, helping teachers and instructional leaders refine their practice. She co-hosts the Jones & McLean podcast.

Opens at bronwynryriejones.com

Emina McLean (opens in a new tab)

Blog · Australia

Australian language and literacy expert who advises school systems on English and literacy, lectures and researches, and is completing a PhD at La Trobe University. Her blog covers best practice in language and literacy teaching, and she co-hosts the Jones & McLean podcast.

Opens at eminamclean.com

Evidence for Learning (opens in a new tab)

Guidance reports · Australia

An Australian organisation that helps educators use evidence well. It is known for its Teaching and Learning Toolkit and guidance reports.

Opens at evidenceforlearning.org.au

Five from Five (opens in a new tab)

Website · Australia

An Australian initiative that promotes effective, evidence-based reading instruction for every child. Its site offers explainers, classroom resources and webinar recordings.

Opens at fivefromfive.com.au

Grattan Institute (opens in a new tab)

Reports · Australia

An independent Australian public policy think tank. Its education program publishes reports on reading, maths, curriculum and school improvement.

Opens at grattan.edu.au

Greg Ashman (opens in a new tab)

Substack · Australia

Australian teacher, writer and podcaster. His Substack, Filling the Pail, makes the case for explicit teaching and takes on common myths about education.

Opens at fillingthepail.substack.com

Haili Hughes (opens in a new tab)

Substack · Australia

Associate Professor at La Trobe University who led in secondary schools in England for 20 years. The author of ten books, she writes about mentoring, coaching and teacher development.

Opens at hailihughes.substack.com

Jamie Clark (opens in a new tab)

One-pagers and newsletter · Australia

Perth-based head of professional growth and author of Teaching One-Pagers. His site offers free one-page guides on the science of learning and links to his DistillED newsletter.

Opens at jamieleeclark.com

Jocelyn Seamer (opens in a new tab)

Blog and podcast · Australia

Australian educator who supports teachers and school leaders with evidence-informed reading and writing instruction. She hosts The Structured Literacy Podcast.

Opens at jocelynseamereducation.com

Learning Difficulties Australia (opens in a new tab)

Association · Australia

A national association of teachers and other professionals dedicated to helping students with learning difficulties through effective, evidence-based teaching.

Opens at ldaustralia.org

Marshall Roberts (opens in a new tab)

Substack · Australia

Tasmanian literacy tutor and chairperson of Code REaD Dyslexia Network. His Substack, Seeking Nisaba, covers dyslexia, diagnosis and Tier 3 intervention, and his writing on the DSM-5 helped prompt the 2025 clarification on diagnosing specific learning disorders.

Opens at marshallroberts.substack.com

Nathaniel Swain (opens in a new tab)

Substack and podcast · Australia

Teacher, instructional coach and La Trobe University researcher who founded Think Forward Educators. He wrote Harnessing the Science of Learning and co-hosts the Chalk Dust podcast.

Opens at nathanielswain.substack.com

NSW Centre for Education Statistics and Evaluation (opens in a new tab)

Research summaries · Australia

The research and evaluation centre of the NSW Department of Education. It produces What Works Best and widely read plain-language summaries, including its papers on cognitive load theory.

Opens at education.nsw.gov.au

Ollie Lovell (opens in a new tab)

Podcast and blog · Australia

Melbourne teacher and host of the Education Research Reading Room podcast. He wrote Sweller's Cognitive Load Theory in Action and Tools for Teachers.

Opens at ollielovell.com

Pamela Snow (opens in a new tab)

Blog · Australia

Distinguished Professor of Cognitive Psychology at La Trobe University and co-director of its Science of Language and Reading (SOLAR) Lab. Her blog, The Snow Report, covers reading instruction, language and the use of evidence in education.

Opens at pamelasnow.blogspot.com

Rebecca Birch (opens in a new tab)

Substack · Australia

Australian educator whose Substack, On Education, looks at evidence-based teaching, literacy and education policy, with an eye on secondary English. She co-hosts the Chalk Dust podcast.

Opens at rebeccabirch.substack.com

Teacher magazine (ACER) (opens in a new tab)

Magazine · Australia

A national online publication for school educators, published by the Australian Council for Educational Research, with articles, podcasts and videos on teaching and leadership.

Opens at teachermagazine.com

Think Forward Educators (opens in a new tab)

Webinars · Australia

An Australian community of teachers and leaders, founded by Nathaniel Swain, that shares the science of learning. It runs webinars, events and networks for educators.

Opens at thinkforwardeducators.org

Trisha Jha (opens in a new tab)

Substack · Australia

Australian education policy researcher who writes Publican Policy, on evidence, school reform and how the science of learning is playing out in Australian policy.

Opens at publicanpolicy.substack.com

Adam Boxer (opens in a new tab)

Substack · UK

Science teacher and Education Director at Carousel. The author of Teaching Secondary Science, he writes about teaching, learning and leading.

Opens at carouselteachlearnlead.substack.com

Alex Quigley (opens in a new tab)

Blog · UK

Former English teacher and author of Closing the Vocabulary Gap, Closing the Reading Gap and Closing the Writing Gap. He blogs about literacy across the curriculum.

Opens at alexquigley.co.uk

Ambition Institute (opens in a new tab)

Papers and blog · UK

An English education charity that trains teachers and school leaders. Its research and insight pages cover teaching, leadership and educational disadvantage.

Opens at ambition.org.uk

Anita Archer (opens in a new tab)

Classroom videos · USA

Co-author of Explicit Instruction: Effective and Efficient Teaching and one of the best-known teachers of explicit instruction. Her site has free classroom videos of the practices in action.

Opens at explicitinstruction.org

Anna Stokke (opens in a new tab)

Podcast · Canada

Mathematics professor at the University of Winnipeg and host of the Chalk & Talk podcast, where she interviews researchers and teachers about evidence-based teaching.

Opens at annastokke.com

Carl Hendrick (opens in a new tab)

Substack · International

A classroom teacher for many years and now a professor at Academica University of Applied Sciences. He co-wrote How Learning Happens with Paul Kirschner and writes The Learning Dispatch.

Opens at carlhendrick.substack.com

Christine Counsell (opens in a new tab)

Blog · UK

History educator and curriculum thinker, and co-creator of the Opening Worlds humanities curriculum. Her blog, The Dignity of the Thing, is widely read on knowledge and curriculum.

Opens at thedignityofthethingblog.wordpress.com

Craig Barton (opens in a new tab)

Substack and podcast · UK

Maths teacher, author of How I Wish I'd Taught Maths and Tips for Teachers, and host of the Mr Barton Maths Podcast. His newsletter shares a classroom tip each week.

Opens at tipsforteachers.substack.com

Daisy Christodoulou (opens in a new tab)

Substack · UK

Director of Education at No More Marking and author of Seven Myths About Education and Making Good Progress? She writes about assessment, writing and technology.

Opens at substack.nomoremarking.com

Daniel Willingham (opens in a new tab)

Articles · USA

Professor of Psychology at the University of Virginia and author of Why Don't Students Like School? He writes about what cognitive science means for the classroom.

Opens at danielwillingham.com

David Didau (opens in a new tab)

Blog and Substack · UK

Former English teacher, now a writer and trainer. He wrote Making Kids Cleverer and What If Everything You Knew About Education Was Wrong?, and blogs at The Learning Spy.

Opens at learningspy.co.uk

Doug Lemov (opens in a new tab)

Blog with video · USA

Author of Teach Like a Champion, which grew from close study of what highly effective teachers do. His blog pairs classroom video with commentary on technique.

Opens at teachlikeachampion.org

Dylan Wiliam (opens in a new tab)

Substack · UK

Former secondary teacher and Emeritus Professor of Educational Assessment at the UCL Institute of Education. He is best known for his work on formative assessment.

Opens at dylanwiliam137385.substack.com

Education Endowment Foundation (opens in a new tab)

Guidance reports · UK

An independent English charity that funds and summarises research on what works in schools. It is known for its Teaching and Learning Toolkit and its guidance reports.

Opens at educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk

Emily Hanford (Sold a Story) (opens in a new tab)

Podcast · USA

American journalist whose podcast Sold a Story investigated how reading came to be taught in ways that ignore the research. It changed the public conversation about reading instruction.

Opens at features.apmreports.org

Holly Korbey (opens in a new tab)

Substack · USA

Education journalist and author. Her newsletter, The Bell Ringer, reports on the science of learning and how it plays out in classrooms.

Opens at hollykorbey.substack.com

International MTSS Association (opens in a new tab)

Association · International

The first professional association dedicated to multi-tiered systems of support, led by founding executive director Joan Schumann. The link opens its MTSS Action Network, a free community with online sessions for teachers, leaders and implementation teams. It also publishes the open-access Journal of Multitiered System of Supports.

Opens at mtssassociation.org

Jim Hewitt and Nidhi Sachdeva (The Science of Learning) (opens in a new tab)

Substack · International

Educators and researchers at the Ontario Institute for Studies in Education, University of Toronto. Their newsletter examines the latest research on the science of learning and what it means for teaching.

Opens at scienceoflearning.substack.com

Joan Sedita (opens in a new tab)

Blog · USA

Founder of Keys to Literacy and creator of The Writing Rope. Her Literacy Lines blog covers writing, comprehension and vocabulary instruction.

Opens at keystoliteracy.com

Josh Goodrich and Steplab (opens in a new tab)

Essays and guides · UK

Josh Goodrich is a co-founder of Steplab, a platform for instructional coaching and professional development, and the author of Responsive Coaching. The Steplab site shares essays and guides on how teachers improve.

Opens at steplab.co

Louisa Moats (opens in a new tab)

Articles and books · USA

American literacy researcher, author of Speech to Print and Teaching Reading Is Rocket Science, and lead author of the LETRS professional learning program.

Opens at louisamoats.com

Mark Seidenberg (opens in a new tab)

Blog · USA

Cognitive scientist and author of Language at the Speed of Sight. He writes about reading research and how it is used, and misused, in classrooms.

Opens at seidenbergreading.net

Mary Myatt (opens in a new tab)

Substack · UK

Education adviser and author of The Curriculum: Gallimaufry to Coherence. Her Substack, Curriculum 101, is about building an ambitious, coherent curriculum.

Opens at marymyatt.substack.com

MTSS Innovations (opens in a new tab)

Association · USA

An American membership organisation for schools putting multi-tiered systems of support in place, co-founded in 2025 by Stephanie Stollar and colleagues. Membership is paid, but this page holds its free discussion guides and webinar recordings.

Opens at mtss-innovations.com

Natalie Wexler (opens in a new tab)

Substack · USA

Education writer, author of The Knowledge Gap and co-author of The Writing Revolution. Her newsletter, Minding the Gap, looks at schooling through the lens of cognitive science.

Opens at nataliewexler.substack.com

Ofsted research reviews (opens in a new tab)

Reviews · UK

Ofsted is England's school inspectorate. This series reviews the research evidence on teaching each curriculum subject.

Opens at gov.uk

Paul Kirschner (opens in a new tab)

Blog and books · International

Emeritus professor of educational psychology at the Open University of the Netherlands and co-author of How Learning Happens. He blogs at 3-Star Learning Experiences.

Opens at 3starlearningexperiences.wordpress.com

Peps Mccrea (opens in a new tab)

Newsletter · UK

Author of the High Impact Teaching series, including Motivated Teaching. His Evidence Snacks newsletter shares one big idea from learning science in a five-minute read each week.

Opens at snacks.pepsmccrea.com

Research Schools Network (opens in a new tab)

Blog and video · UK

A network of schools in England, supported by the Education Endowment Foundation, that helps other schools put evidence into practice. Its blog and videos show what that looks like.

Opens at researchschool.org.uk

Retrieval Practice (Pooja Agarwal) (opens in a new tab)

Downloads · USA

Cognitive scientist Pooja Agarwal, co-author of Powerful Teaching, shares free guides and resources on retrieval practice and other research-backed strategies.

Opens at retrievalpractice.org

Sarah Powell (opens in a new tab)

Research and resources · USA

Professor at the University of Texas at Austin who researches maths difficulties and intervention. Her site shares free resources on word problems and maths intervention.

Opens at sarahpowellphd.com

The Learning Scientists (opens in a new tab)

Blog and downloads · USA

A group of cognitive psychologists who make research on learning accessible to teachers and students. They are known for their six strategies for effective learning.

Opens at learningscientists.org

The Reading League (opens in a new tab)

Guides and video · USA

An American non-profit that promotes the science of reading. It publishes the Science of Reading: Defining Guide and a large library of free talks.

Opens at thereadingleague.org

The Writing Revolution (Judith Hochman) (opens in a new tab)

Writing instruction · USA

The organisation founded by Judith Hochman to share the Hochman Method of explicit writing instruction, set out in The Writing Revolution, which she wrote with Natalie Wexler.

Opens at thewritingrevolution.org

Timothy Shanahan (opens in a new tab)

Blog · USA

Distinguished Professor Emeritus at the University of Illinois Chicago and a member of the US National Reading Panel. His blog answers teachers' questions about reading research and instruction.

Opens at shanahanonliteracy.com

Tom Sherrington (opens in a new tab)

Blog · UK

Former headteacher who now works with schools on teaching and curriculum. He wrote Rosenshine's Principles in Action, co-created the Teaching WalkThrus series and blogs at Teacherhead.

Opens at teacherhead.com

Zach Groshell (opens in a new tab)

Blog and podcast · USA

Teacher, instructional coach and author of Just Tell Them, a guide to explicit instruction. He writes at Education Rickshaw and hosts the Progressively Incorrect podcast.

Opens at educationrickshaw.com
