About this collection

Other people's brilliant work, gathered in one place

I'm Jo Griffin. I was spending a long time trawling the internet to find resources that support teachers' professional learning, and thought it might help others if they were all in one place, sorted into categories.

This is a collection, not my own work. Every resource belongs to the people who created it. Each entry names its creator and links straight to the original, and nothing is copied or hosted here.

Is your work listed here? If you would like it described differently or removed, just let me know.

Help it grow

Know something that belongs here?

This library is built by sharing. If there is a video, podcast, article or piece of research that has helped you or your team, send a quick email with the link and it can be added for everyone.
