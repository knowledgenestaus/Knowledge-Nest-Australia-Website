Research 2

CATALISE: A multinational and multidisciplinary Delphi consensus study. Identifying language impairments in children (opens in a new tab)

Bishop, Snowling, Thompson, Greenhalgh and the CATALISE consortium · 2016 · Consensus study

The open-access consensus study in which an international expert panel worked towards agreed criteria for identifying children's language difficulties. It underpins the shift to the term developmental language disorder.

FreeOpens at journals.plos.org

Snowling, Hulme and Nation · 2020 · Review article

An open-access review that describes dyslexia as a difficulty in learning to decode and spell, places it among the neurodevelopmental disorders, and traces how understanding of it has changed.
