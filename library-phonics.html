Phonics

How to teach the links between letters and sounds systematically, so children can decode words for reading and encode them for spelling. Here you can watch phonics lessons being taught, step by step, and find webinars, podcast episodes and guides on systematic phonics, decodable texts and what to do after the Phonics Screening Check.
