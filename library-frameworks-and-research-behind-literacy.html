Literacy

Frameworks and research behind literacy

Good literacy teaching rests on a handful of big ideas about how children learn to read, write and use language. I've gathered the frameworks and the research behind them here, so you can see where our practice comes from and explain it with confidence.

The Simple View of Reading 1

Reading comprehension is the product of decoding and language comprehension, so a reader needs both, and a weakness in either one limits understanding. Duke and Cartwright's Active View of Reading (2021) extends it, adding the reader's active self-regulation and the skills that bridge word recognition and language comprehension.

Syntheses of the field 11

The major reviews, inquiries and reports that pull the reading research together, from the National Reading Panel and Australia's 2005 National Inquiry to Ending the Reading Wars. Structured literacy sits here too, meaning explicit, systematic and cumulative teaching of how language works, from sounds and spellings through to word parts, sentences and meaning.
