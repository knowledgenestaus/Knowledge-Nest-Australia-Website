Tools: Free digital tools for teaching

Free digital tools you can open and use in a lesson or while planning. There are dictionaries, word origin and word part tools, virtual maths manipulatives, practice generators, timers and name pickers. Each one is free to use, and I have said so where a free account or advertising is involved. Every entry links to the original.

Jump to:Words and meaningsWord origins and word partsPhonics and word buildingChecking a textMaths manipulativesMaths practice and graphingTimers, pickers and classroom screens

Words and meanings 4

Dictionaries that explain words in plain language, for planning student-friendly definitions.

Collins COBUILD dictionary (opens in a new tab)

By Collins · Words and meanings

Definitions written as full sentences in everyday language, which makes them a good model for student-friendly explanations of new words.

FreeOpens at collinsdictionary.com

Longman Dictionary of Contemporary English (opens in a new tab)

By Pearson Longman · Words and meanings

Definitions written using a controlled set of about 2,000 common words, with example sentences. Useful when planning how to explain a word simply.

FreeOpens at ldoceonline.com

Cambridge Dictionary (opens in a new tab)

By Cambridge University Press · Words and meanings

Clear definitions with British spelling, example sentences and recorded pronunciations.

FreeOpens at dictionary.cambridge.org

Wordsmyth children's dictionary (opens in a new tab)

By Wordsmyth · Words and meanings

A dictionary written for primary students, with simple definitions, pictures and word parts. The free version carries advertising.

FreeOpens at kids.wordsmyth.net

Word origins and word parts 3

Where words come from and how they are built, for morphology, etymology and spelling lessons.

Spelfabet spelling lists (opens in a new tab)

By Alison Clarke, Spelfabet · Word origins and word parts · Australia

An Australian speech pathologist's word lists sorted by sound and by spelling pattern. Use them to choose words for phonics and spelling practice.

FreeOpens at spelfabet.com.au

Online Etymology Dictionary (opens in a new tab)

By Douglas Harper · Word origins and word parts

Look up where a word came from and how its meaning and spelling changed over time. A quick check when planning morphology and spelling lessons.

FreeOpens at etymonline.com

Word root dictionary (opens in a new tab)

By LearnThatWord · Word origins and word parts

A long list of Greek and Latin roots, prefixes and suffixes with their meanings and example words.

FreeOpens at learnthat.org

Phonics and word building 1

On-screen letter tiles and boards for blending, segmenting and word chains.

UFLI Foundations apps: Blending Board and Word Work Mats (opens in a new tab)

By University of Florida Literacy Institute · Phonics and word building

Free on-screen letter tiles for blending drills and word chains. Made for UFLI Foundations lessons but usable with any phonics sequence.

FreeOpens at ufli.education.ufl.edu

Checking a text 1

Quick checks on how hard a passage is likely to be.

Lexile Text Analyzer (opens in a new tab)

By MetaMetrics · Checking a text

Paste in a passage to estimate its difficulty. A rough guide only, since a readability score says nothing about the knowledge a text assumes.

FreeFree account neededOpens at hub.lexile.com

Maths manipulatives 5

Virtual versions of counters, ten frames, base ten blocks, fraction models and number lines, to project while you model.

Math Learning Center apps (opens in a new tab)

By The Math Learning Center · Maths manipulatives

Free virtual manipulatives that run in a browser or on a tablet, including number frames, number lines, number racks, base ten pieces, fraction models, geoboards, clocks and money.

FreeOpens at mathlearningcenter.org

Polypad (opens in a new tab)

By Amplify (Mathigon) · Maths manipulatives

One blank canvas with fraction bars, algebra tiles, number lines, base ten blocks, geometry tools and more. Good for projecting a representation while you model.

FreeOpens at polypad.amplify.com

Didax virtual manipulatives (opens in a new tab)

By Didax · Maths manipulatives

Browser versions of common classroom materials, including Unifix cubes, ten frames, place value disks, base ten blocks, fraction tiles and a rekenrek, with no login.

FreeOpens at didax.com

Toy Theater virtual manipulatives (opens in a new tab)

By Toy Theater · Maths manipulatives

Simple, colourful manipulatives for the early years, including counters, ten frames, hundreds charts, fraction strips and clocks.

FreeOpens at toytheater.com

MathsBot (opens in a new tab)

By Jonathan Hall · Maths manipulatives

A United Kingdom teacher's free collection of virtual manipulatives, question generators, starters and printable practice.

FreeOpens at mathsbot.com

Maths practice and graphing 6

Ready-made daily review, fact practice, diagnostic questions and graphing.

Hit the Button (opens in a new tab)

By Topmarks · Maths practice and graphing

Quick-fire practice of number bonds, doubles, halves, times tables and division facts against the clock.

FreeOpens at topmarks.co.uk

Corbettmaths 5-a-day (opens in a new tab)

By John Corbett · Maths practice and graphing

Five mixed review questions for every day of the year at several levels, with answers. Ready-made daily review for upper primary and secondary.

FreeOpens at corbettmaths.com

Diagnostic Questions (opens in a new tab)

By Craig Barton and Eedi · Maths practice and graphing

Thousands of multiple-choice maths questions in which each wrong answer points to a particular misconception. A source of hinge questions.

FreeFree account neededOpens at diagnosticquestions.com

Variation Theory (opens in a new tab)

By Craig Barton · Maths practice and graphing

Sequences of carefully varied maths questions, shared by teachers, that draw attention to one change at a time.

FreeOpens at variationtheory.com

Desmos graphing calculator (opens in a new tab)

By Desmos Studio · Maths practice and graphing

A free graphing calculator for the screen, with sliders for showing how a graph changes.

FreeOpens at desmos.com

GeoGebra (opens in a new tab)

By GeoGebra · Maths practice and graphing

Free geometry, graphing and 3D tools, plus a large library of ready-made classroom activities.

FreeOpens at geogebra.org

Timers, pickers and classroom screens 4

Small helpers for routines: Countdowns, random name pickers and a screen for instructions.

Classroomscreen (opens in a new tab)

By Classroomscreen · Timers, pickers and classroom screens

Put a timer, a noise meter, a random name picker and written instructions on one projected screen. The basic version is free.

FreeOpens at classroomscreen.com

Online Stopwatch classroom timers (opens in a new tab)

By Online Stopwatch · Timers, pickers and classroom screens

Countdown timers to project for transitions, quiet work and timed practice. Free, with advertising.

FreeOpens at online-stopwatch.com

Wheel of Names (opens in a new tab)

By Wheel of Names · Timers, pickers and classroom screens

Type in your class list and spin to choose a student. One way to run cold call so that every student expects to be asked.

FreeOpens at wheelofnames.com

Flippity (opens in a new tab)

By Flippity · Timers, pickers and classroom screens

Turns a spreadsheet into flashcards, a random name picker, a quiz or a matching game.

FreeOpens at flippity.net
